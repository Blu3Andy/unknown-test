Defect detection

Which defects does Quins Ultimate detect?

Quins Ultimate supports inspection for optically visible deviations on PCBs, assemblies, components, solder joints, paste and coatings.

Component and placement defects

  • Missing or extra components
  • Wrong components
  • Rotated or reverse-polarity components
  • Shifted or tombstoned components
  • Bent pins
  • Visibly damaged components
  • Wrong PCB or assembly revision

Solder and paste defects

  • Solder bridges
  • Unsoldered or visibly faulty solder joints
  • Lifted leads
  • Missing solder paste
  • Too much or too little solder paste
  • Smeared paste
  • Paste bridges

PCB defects

  • Visible trace breaks
  • Traces too narrow or too wide
  • Incorrect, oversized or undersized drill holes
  • Deviations in solder mask
  • Contamination and visible damage

Conformal coating defects

With Quins Ultimate UV, the following deviations can be made visible, among others:
  • Missing conformal coating
  • Too little or too much coating
  • Coating splatter
  • Uneven coating
  • Bubbling
  • Deviations in conformal coating
The system inspects optically accessible features that are captured in the inspection image. Hidden solder joints or electrical function faults require complementary test methods.
How it works

How inspection with Quins Ultimate works

1. Capture reference

An approved assembly or golden board is captured and stored as the reference.

2. Identify the unit under test

The unit under test can be uniquely assigned to a project, order or batch, for example via a barcode.

3. Capture and compare the image

The system creates a reproducible image capture. Inspection is carried out manually via image-difference inspection or automatically with the appropriate software module.

4. Mark deviations

Deviations found are marked directly in the inspection image and can be forwarded to a repair station.

5. Document results

Inspection images, evaluations, repairs and further information are stored for documentation, statistics and traceability.

System comparison

Quins Ultimate or Quins Pro?

Quins Ultimate is the right solution when a fully integrated, mobile inspection station is needed. The system can be used flexibly between different workstations or production areas.
Quins Pro is particularly well suited to a stationary inspection station. For true line integration and special component-height requirements, other QUINS systems such as Quins Flex Line are available.
