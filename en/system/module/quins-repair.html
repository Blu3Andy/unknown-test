Digital repair management

How QUINS Repair supports structured rework

QUINS Repair supports structured rework of PCBs and assemblies by automatically transferring defects captured during inspection to the designated repair station. There, the operator receives all required information for processing, including defect position, reference position, defect image, component data, and current inspection status depending on system configuration.
QUINS Repair does not detect defects itself. The module takes over defects already captured in the QUINS inspection process – for example from Quins Easy, Quins Easy Plus, Quins AIP, or Quins UVI – and makes them available for structured rework and documentation.

Rework workflow

From defect capture to documented rework

In many production lines, defects are first identified during inspection and then manually handed over to repair stations. During this handover, information is often lost or has to be searched again. QUINS Repair provides a digital workflow for structured rework, keeping the detected defect available at the repair station with image, position, component information, and status.
This reduces search effort, prevents information loss, and ensures repair decisions are documented in a traceable way.
Quality and traceability

Repair data for quality assurance and traceability

Every repair decision creates valuable data for quality assurance. QUINS Repair documents whether a defect was repaired, partially repaired, classified as a pseudo defect, forwarded for re-inspection, or marked as reject.
Together with QUINS View and QUINS Statistics, this data can be used for documentation, defect history, quality analysis, and full traceability of PCB and assembly rework.
Repair workflow

From detected defect to documented repair.

Quins Repair connects inspection and rework without media breaks. Every defect follows a clearly traceable path – from capture in the inspection process to a documented repair decision.

Capture the defect during inspection

A defect is marked with Quins Easy or Easy Plus, or reported automatically by an extension such as AIP or UVI.

Hand the defect to the repair order

The defect is assigned to the repair order together with the available project, image, and component data.

Identify the assembly

The operator calls up the assembly manually, via the order, serial number, or optionally by barcode.

Display the defect position

Quins Repair shows the affected position on the assembly and compares it against a reference position or reference image.

Repair and document the result

The operator repairs the position and classifies the outcome as repaired, partially repaired, pseudo defect, or reject. Depending on the process, a re-inspection or release follows.
Visible at the repair station

What information does Quins Repair show?

Depending on the available project and inspection data, repair staff get all relevant information right at the workstation:
  • Defect position – the position of the marked defect is highlighted within the assembly image.
  • Reference position – comparison against a defect-free reference or golden board.
  • Defect image – the image captured during inspection helps locate and assess the deviation.
  • Component information – e.g. designator, part number, component description, footprint, comment, target position.
  • Repair status – open, started, completed, or forwarded for re-inspection.
  • Order and unit data – e.g. order number, project, serial number, inspection time.
Clear outcomes

Repair decisions, explained clearly.

Every processed defect is closed with a clear outcome. Only status values that actually exist in the relevant system configuration are used.

Repaired

The defect was fully repaired; the assembly can be forwarded for re-inspection or release.

Partial repair

Some of the marked defects have been repaired while other positions remain open.

Pseudo defect

The original report is classified as not an actual product defect and is assigned to a user and a timestamp.

Reject

The assembly or position is marked as not economically or technically repairable.

For re-inspection

Processing is complete; depending on the defined process, the unit must be checked again.
