What is Quins Repair?

Quins Repair is the repair module within the QUINS software environment. Defects already captured during inspection are automatically handed over to the designated repair station. There, the operator receives all the information needed for the job – depending on configuration, this includes the defect position, a reference position, the related defect image, component information, and the previous inspection status.
Quins Repair does not detect defects itself. The module takes over defects already captured in the QUINS inspection process – for example from Quins Easy, Quins Easy Plus, Quins AIP, or Quins UVI – and supports their structured processing and documentation at the repair station.
Repair workflow

From detected defect to documented repair.

Quins Repair connects inspection and rework without media breaks. Every defect follows a clearly traceable path – from capture in the inspection process to a documented repair decision.

Capture the defect during inspection

A defect is marked with Quins Easy or Easy Plus, or reported automatically by an extension such as AIP or UVI.

Hand the defect to the repair order

The defect is assigned to the repair order together with the available project, image, and component data.

Identify the assembly

The operator calls up the assembly manually, via the order, serial number, or optionally by barcode.

Display the defect position

Quins Repair shows the affected position on the assembly and compares it against a reference position or reference image.

Repair and document the result

The operator repairs the position and classifies the outcome as repaired, partially repaired, pseudo defect, or reject. Depending on the process, a re-inspection or release follows.
Visible at the repair station

What information does Quins Repair show?

Depending on the available project and inspection data, repair staff get all relevant information right at the workstation:
  • Defect position – the position of the marked defect is highlighted within the assembly image.
  • Reference position – comparison against a defect-free reference or golden board.
  • Defect image – the image captured during inspection helps locate and assess the deviation.
  • Component information – e.g. designator, part number, component description, footprint, comment, target position.
  • Repair status – open, started, completed, or forwarded for re-inspection.
  • Order and unit data – e.g. order number, project, serial number, inspection time.
Clear outcomes

Repair decisions, explained clearly.

Every processed defect is closed with a clear outcome. Only status values that actually exist in the relevant system configuration are used.

Repaired

The defect was fully repaired; the assembly can be forwarded for re-inspection or release.

Partial repair

Some of the marked defects have been repaired while other positions remain open.

Pseudo defect

The original report is classified as not an actual product defect and is assigned to a user and a timestamp.

Reject

The assembly or position is marked as not economically or technically repairable.

For re-inspection

Processing is complete; depending on the defined process, the unit must be checked again.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Quins Repair

What is Quins Repair?

Quins Repair is a software module for the guided processing and documentation of defects already detected on PCBs and electronic assemblies.

Does Quins Repair detect defects itself?

No. Defects are detected or marked in an upstream inspection process. Quins Repair takes over this information and makes it available at the repair station.

What information is shown at the repair station?

Depending on the available data, this can include defect position, reference position, defect image, component description, designator, part number, footprint, and further project information.

How is the correct assembly called up?

Depending on configuration, the repair order can be called up manually, via an order ID, serial number, or barcode.

Is Quins Repair suitable for SMT and THT?

Yes, the module can be used to process defects on SMT, THT, and mixed assemblies.

What do pseudo defect and partial repair mean?

A pseudo defect is a report that, after review by the operator, is not confirmed as an actual product defect. In a partial repair, individual defects within an order are processed while other defect positions remain open.

Is a re-inspection possible?

Depending on the defined workflow, a repaired unit can be handed back to a QUINS inspection station for another check.

Are repair statistics available?

Quins Repair provides the underlying repair data. Cross-process statistics and quality analyses are primarily created with Quins Statistics.

Does Quins Repair support ISO 9001 or ISO 13485?

The module can support documented repair processes within a quality management system. How it fits into specific standards needs to be assessed on a customer-by-customer basis.

Can Quins Repair be connected to ERP or MES systems?

According to the product information, an external database interface is available. The specific interfaces and supported systems are agreed on a project-by-project basis.
