What is Quins Easy?

Optical inspection software for circuit boards and assemblies

Quins Easy is the base software of the QUINS inspection systems. It combines high-resolution image acquisition of QUINS hardware with an intuitive environment for optical inspection. Reference and test object are automatically centered and displayed in rapid alternation. Deviations between the two images appear as clearly visible movement.
The software is suitable for incoming goods inspection, production, and outgoing goods inspection, as well as for first-article inspection, sampling, setup control, and series inspections. The inspector evaluates the discernible deviations and can document, track, and evaluate the results depending on the module used.

Alternating Image Inspection

How alternating image inspection works

Detect errors faster through direct image comparison. In alternating image inspection, reference image and test object are automatically centered and displayed alternately. Different positions and features appear as movement or blinking.
6 steps to inspection results:
1
Capture reference – a defect-free assembly is stored as reference image
2
Capture test object – the circuit board to be inspected is captured in high resolution
3
Align images – Quins Easy automatically centers reference and test object
4
Make deviations visible – rapid image switching shows differences as movement
5
Evaluate result – inspector marks found defects
6
Document inspection – with expansion modules store and evaluate results

What errors can be detected?

Systematically detect visually apparent deviations

Quins Easy supports the inspection staff in identifying visually apparent deviations. Which errors can be reliably assessed depends on image resolution, lighting, component geometry, and hardware used.
Component errors: missing, wrong or extra components · displaced, rotated or reversed components · upright components or damaged components · bent pins · tombstone effect
Solder joint errors: solder bridges · unsoldered or incomplete solder joints · solder joint lugs · visibly damaged solder joints
Paste errors: missing, too little or too much paste · smeared paste or paste bridges
PCB deviations: wrong PCB version · conductor path interruption · wrong or too large/small drill holes · contamination or damage
Conformal coating and protection errors: missing or too little/much coating · coating splatters or bubble formation · uneven coating
Note: Quins Easy is intended for visually apparent features. Hidden solder joints, internal PCB errors, or electrical function cannot be assessed by optical inspection alone. Depending on the application, supplementary methods such as ICT, functional testing, or X-ray inspection may be required.
